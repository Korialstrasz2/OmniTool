Output and recovery boundaries
Matching sidecars use the audio stem; .lrc takes precedence over .txt. Plain lyrics are embedded; synchronized LRC is also saved beside the new copy. Failed, unmatched, and protected tracks are not copied. Provider matches are reviewed, not assumed correct.
The output must be a new directory outside the source tree, with an existing parent. Originals provide the recovery path: discard unwanted copies manually. Force-stopping may leave a .omnitool-lyrics-*.partial staging folder. Native tag parsing is process-isolated but is not an audited sandbox. Do not feed untrusted audio files.
Install requirements-content.txt explicitly. Automatic AI metadata recovery and the old unencrypted provider have been removed. Nothing is automatically published or committed to Git.
